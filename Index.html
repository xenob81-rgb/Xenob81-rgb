The thinking behind PULSE.

PULSE explores how a video player can become part of a brand's visual identity rather than simply a functional component. Its deep charcoal surfaces create contrast, while the coral accent draws attention to important playback actions. Rounded controls, clear visual hierarchy and responsive spacing create a consistent experience across screen sizes.
